Understanding Color Mixing Models

Subtractive (RYB / Paint)

Used with physical pigments and paints. Colors are formed by absorbing (subtracting) wavelengths of light. Mixing more colors together makes the result darker. Primary colors: Red, Yellow, Blue. Mixing all primaries → near black/brown. Used by painters, printmakers, and traditional artists.

Additive (RGB / Light)

Used with light sources like screens and LEDs. Colors are formed by adding (combining) wavelengths of light. Mixing more colors makes the result brighter. Primary colors: Red, Green, Blue. Mixing all primaries → white. Used in monitors, projectors, and stage lighting.
